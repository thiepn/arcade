Baseline: `f9bb9efe26994bb821a10e7908ae794fd09f9059` (production-certified P22).

## Purpose

P23 transforms the seven immutable P15 B-rank games into current S-rank candidates without changing the P15 record, lowering the 55/60 threshold, or adding replay-recording/retention/metagame systems. It is the final game-transformation phase; **P24 remains a separate definitive 32/32 certification**.

Automation certifies objective source/runtime contracts. It does not prove subjective fun or replace physical-device experiential acceptance.

## Transformation 1 — Type Rush / Directive Relays

The four certified BOOT / SURGE / OVERCLOCK / REDLINE waves remain unchanged in spawn cadence, simultaneous-word ceiling and speed. P23 adds two authored relay identities per wave. Completing a real target commits CONTROL or VOLATILE based on the target's existing special/urgency state; later steps require existing STANDARD, SPECIAL, URGENT or RISK conditions. Relay completion is score-only.

Ordinary typing, auto-lock, direct target selection, bomb/freeze/hyper behavior and danger-line failure remain authoritative.

## Transformation 2 — Perfect Stop / Beacon Routes

All seven core sectors and three Master Encore sectors remain. Core sectors now render a non-overlapping optional precision beacon in addition to the ordinary target. Hitting the ordinary or precision beacon commits a short CALIBRATION or EDGE route using the same single stop action. A precision GREAT/PERFECT may add one extra Master credit; the ordinary target remains a valid route to the original qualification threshold.

No timing window is widened and no life/survival rule changes.

## Transformation 3 — Reaction / Reaction Circuits

All eight core configurations and all three overtime configurations remain. Three authored pairs can be ordered as SPEED or CONTROL. Selection occurs only on a completed RESULT screen, never during WAITING, DECOY or READY. A clean two-round pair adds score. Overtime qualification and the P16 combined session lookup remain intact.

## Transformation 4 — Pulse / Groove Paths

The six existing groove patterns remain unchanged and are composed into four authored four-beat paths: FLOW, DRIVE, ECHO and REDLINE. A/D or Left/Right queues the next branch; touch buttons provide parity. Tap/Space remains the timing action and F/Shift remains Sync Wager.

The 155 BPM cap, 8/18/28px judgements, two-charge wager cap, starting charge, earn-every-four-combo rule and ±10px wager window remain unchanged.

## Transformation 5 — Laser Rope Reflex / Choreographies

LOW, HIGH and DUAL remain the only beam modes. Four authored choreography sequences compose them. HIGH remains unavailable until its existing streak threshold and DUAL remains unavailable until streak 12. If the desired choreography step is not yet eligible, the game waits within the existing grammar instead of bypassing progression.

Every applied mode transition still calls the P16 geometric warning guard. `LASER_ROPE_MODE_MIN_WARNING_SEC` remains 0.38. Redline remains earn-every-5, max-2, 4 seconds, ×1.22 speed and ×2 score.

## Transformation 6 — Aero Pulse / Flight Lines

RISE, FALL, WEAVE and LEVEL are three-gate authored lines. Their gate centers stay within a deterministic bounded center-delta envelope and can be mirrored. HIGH, CENTER and LOW trace markers sit inside the ordinary safe gap. A player chooses a trace solely through normal flap altitude; completing one trace across the line adds score.

Gate spacing remains 200–240px, gap height remains at least 90px, base speed remains capped at 280px/s, and Flow remains max-2 / start-1 / earn-every-3-grazes / 4 seconds / ×1.18 speed / ×2 score. Moving gates move the P23 traces by the same vertical delta as their gap.

## Transformation 7 — Stack / Tower Blueprints

Stack remains one-tap placement plus the existing optional Focus wager. P23 classifies real overlap geometry into centered, left/right offset, narrow overlap, perfect and Focus-perfect results. Five optional blueprints—CENTERLINE, OFFSET, BRIDGE, FOCUS SPIRE and SKYLINE—request short placement relationships. Branchable blueprints mirror from the player's actual first left/right offset.

Missing a blueprint only resets optional mastery. Width loss, no-overlap game-over, one-tap placement, speed progression and Focus rules are unchanged. The P16 3.5 base, +0.08 per physical block, +4.5 additive cap and P14/P16 Focus 4px/2px windows remain authoritative.

## Adversarial review summary

- **Type Rush:** Relays must remain tied to real target completion; no RNG-dependent required type can become a survival condition.
- **Perfect Stop:** Agency 9 depends on target choice and downstream route/Encore consequence, not merely a second colored beacon.
- **Reaction:** Circuit selection must remain completely outside live reaction windows.
- **Pulse:** Path choice must not alter timing judgement or become confused with Sync Wager/tap input.
- **Laser Rope Reflex:** Choreography must remain inside LOW/HIGH/DUAL and the 0.38s warning contract.
- **Aero Pulse:** Flight traces must stay inside legal gaps and never become collision requirements.
- **Stack:** Blueprints must classify actual geometry; missing mastery cannot alter ordinary stack legality.

## P18/P19 presentation boundary

`p23ClarityProfileExtensions.ts` extends the seven existing P18 profiles with P23 mastery language. Pulse additionally receives an updated P23 `sourceControls` contract for A/D or Left/Right Groove Path selection plus Sync Wager. `p23TransformationRuntime.ts` installs those control/teaching extensions before play and appends mastery guidance only into the canonical P18/P19 pause surface.

## Manual acceptance boundary

For every transformed game, manual acceptance should cover first-run clarity, transformation use/ignore paths, skilled use, high-pressure readability, mobile/touch, reduced motion, pause/restart/exit, and the question: *does a fresh run differ because of gameplay decisions rather than retention mechanics?*

Automation does not manufacture subjective answers to those questions.
